# Milestone 2 Review Report — Career Evolution Loop v1

## What we learned

- The completed slices form a coherent companion loop when ordered around the user's understanding: evidence first, then one direction, a source-linked snapshot, a single reflection, and one confidence-gated possibility.
- The user does not repeat resume information. The only inputs are the three existing bounded responses; omitting direction records a skip and retains uncertainty.
- A possibility is meaningful only when the snapshot has both a user-confirmed direction and supported strengths. Otherwise the loop explains why it cannot introduce one.

## Review findings

Human review remains pending. The integrated HTML and JSON artifacts preserve existing evidence boundaries and do not introduce inference or Candidate Knowledge writes. The completion section makes the current limit of understanding visible rather than presenting a career conclusion.
