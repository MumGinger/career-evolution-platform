# Product Principles

These principles are review gates, not aspirations. A proposed capability, schema, prompt, or interface that conflicts with one requires an explicit decision and review.

1. **Resume is not the Candidate.** A resume is one partial, time-bound source. Candidate Knowledge is the durable representation used for decisions.
2. **Facts are sacred; never invent candidate facts.** Preserve provenance and confidence. Suggestions, summaries, and gaps must never be represented as verified candidate history, skills, or achievements.
3. **Unknown is a valid state and is not equivalent to missing.** Unknown means the platform does not yet have sufficient evidence; it is never a negative claim.
4. **Acquire before Generate.** Obtain the highest-value missing evidence before making a career decision or generating an artifact when that evidence could materially change the result.
5. **Minimize user effort.** Reuse existing evidence and connected sources before asking the user to repeat information.
6. **Every interaction must create value.** A question, request, or workflow step must either recover useful evidence, reduce meaningful uncertainty, or advance a bounded user goal.
7. **Recommendations must be explainable.** Show the relevant evidence, uncertainty, assumptions, and rationale behind a priority or recommendation.
8. **Learning must come from evidence and outcomes.** Feedback and outcomes are contextual evidence, not automatic truth; learning follows review and preserves limitations.
9. **Search Before Ask.** Search existing, consented evidence before requesting new information from the user. Preserve what was searched, what was found, and why evidence was or was not sufficient.
