# AI Career Companion Manifesto

## Our mission

> Help people discover, develop, and realize the value they can create in the world.

A career is not merely a sequence of applications or documents. It is one expression of a meaningful life. We exist to help people see themselves more clearly and make their own next move.

## The companion we are becoming

The platform evolves with the user's needs:

**Resume Builder → Career Assistant → Career Coach → Career Thinking Partner → Life-long Career Companion**

Each stage adds understanding and care. The destination is not an AI that directs a person's career; it is a trusted companion that helps them direct it for themselves.

## Our principles

1. **We never pretend to know the user.** We distinguish what has been observed from what remains uncertain.
2. **We earn every question.** We ask only when the answer can create future value, and the user can always decline.
3. **We learn gradually.** We build a relationship through useful interactions, not a static profile assembled all at once.
4. **We explain why.** Any understanding we present should be grounded in what led to it and clear about its confidence.
5. **Uncertainty is a feature.** Unknowns stay unknown; we do not manufacture certainty to make an interaction feel complete.
6. **We observe before we advise.** Advice without understanding is opinion. We first listen, notice, and reflect.
7. **We guide; we do not decide.** A user's career belongs to them. Our role is to expand perspective, not take control.
8. **Questions are often more valuable than answers.** The right question can help a person recognize possibilities that a recommendation would hide.
9. **Every interaction should help the user understand themselves a little better.** Even a small exchange should leave them with more clarity, agency, or language for their experience.
10. **Success is measured by human growth, not resume count or application count.** Useful outcomes include clearer self-understanding, better decisions, and durable confidence.
11. **The best AI increases the user's independence over time.** We succeed when users become more able to reflect, choose, and act without us.
12. **A lifelong career companion should expand a person's thinking before expanding their options.** We offer perspective before pathways, and possibility before recommendation.

## An engineering commitment

Technical correctness is necessary, but it is not sufficient. Future pull requests should be evaluated against this manifesto as well: do they preserve user agency, earn their questions, communicate uncertainty, and help people understand themselves more clearly?
