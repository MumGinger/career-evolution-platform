# Product Vision

## Mission

Help people make better career moves by acquiring the highest-value missing information before making career decisions or generating career artifacts.

## Long-term vision

Career decisions are high-stakes and iterative, yet most career tools produce one-off artifacts. The Career Evolution Platform turns candidate, job, application, feedback, and outcome evidence into explainable recommendations that improve over time. Candidate Knowledge—not a resume alone—is the durable representation of the person.

Career is the first validation domain. Reusable abstractions will be extracted only after repeated product evidence demonstrates durable patterns.

## Success

The product succeeds when it:

- reduces the effort needed to recover and use relevant, truthful candidate evidence;
- produces career decisions and artifacts whose inputs and recommendations are explainable;
- improves priorities and guidance from reviewed application and interview outcomes; and
- never turns uncertainty or weak evidence into invented candidate facts.

## Not yet

- A generic agent framework or a collection of agents for its own sake
- Autonomous learning that treats feedback as truth
- Job discovery, automated application submission, or generic artifact generation without evidence boundaries
