# Vision

## Why this exists

Career decisions are high-stakes and iterative, yet most career tools produce one-off artifacts. They rarely learn from outcomes such as recruiter feedback, interview performance, offer results, or a user's evolving goals.

The Career Evolution Platform aims to help people make better career moves over time by turning relevant real-world signals into carefully reviewed improvements.

## Core hypothesis

An evidence-based evolution loop can improve future career outputs when it captures context, weighs evidence rather than blindly obeying feedback, and updates skills through deliberate review.

## What we are building

An AI-powered career product whose recommendations and artifacts can improve across applications, interviews, feedback, and outcomes.

## What we are not building yet

- A generic agent framework
- A collection of agents for its own sake
- A one-shot resume generator optimized only for prompt quality
- Autonomous learning that treats all feedback as ground truth

Career is the first validation domain. Generalizable mechanisms will be extracted only when repeated product evidence demonstrates they are truly reusable.
