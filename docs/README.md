# Product Documentation

These documents are the product's durable source of intent and governance. Read them before changing behavior; do not rely on chat history as the record of a decision.

## Start here

The [AI Career Companion Manifesto](vision/ai-career-companion-manifesto.md) records the long-term philosophy that guides product decisions.

1. [Vision](vision/vision.md) — mission, long-term direction, and success.
2. [Product principles](principles/product-principles.md) — rules every capability must respect.
3. [Roadmap](roadmap/roadmap.md) and [current state](current-state.md) — delivery sequence and current focus.
4. [System overview](architecture/system-overview.md) — the product information flow.
5. [Glossary](glossary.md) — canonical domain terms.

## Governance records

- [ADRs](adr/README.md) record durable decisions and their consequences.
- [PRDs](prd/README.md) define capability-level product behavior and scope.
- [Implementation and review reports](../reports/) record what was delivered and what still needs human review.

Design documents in `design/` describe supporting domain models; `specs/` contains implementation specifications for already selected delivery work.
